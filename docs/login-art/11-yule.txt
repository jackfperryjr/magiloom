SCENE 11 — YULE  (key: yule, holiday scene, night only)
=======================================================

SUBJECT
A stand of firs on a still winter night, with the mascot standing in the snow.

Deep snow lies along the upper surface of every bough, so each tier reads as a
dark branch with a white load on top. More firs behind, smaller and bluer with
distance. A drifted snow bank runs across the foreground, closing over the base
of the nearest trees. Warm light spills in from somewhere off-frame to the
right — a window or a doorway you never see — laying a long soft wedge of gold
across the snow. Fine snow still falling, sparse, and a scatter of stars in the
gaps between the crowns.

THE MASCOT
At about 83% across the frame, standing in the snow with his feet near the
bottom edge and about a third of the frame tall: a small round-limbed ragdoll
made of stitched amber-gold cloth (#e8a72c body, #c98720 in shadow, warm
#f7cf6a highlights, dark brown stitching), with a large round head nearly half
his total height, visible seams along his limbs, and a soft sheen high on one
side of the head. Soft, hand-sewn, a little lopsided — a toy, not a creature.

HE HAS NO EYES AND NO FACE. His only feature is a short row of stitch ticks low
on the head, curving slightly, which reads as a small closed smile. Nothing
else is marked on the head at all — no eyes, no button eyes, no nose, no brows.

He is cheering. Both arms are flung straight up over his head, his body leans
back a little, and one foot is lifted as though caught mid-bounce — turned
three-quarters toward the warm light, delighted by it. He wears a red santa hat
with a white fur band, knocked off-centre by the motion, its bobble trailing out
to one side.

The light catches him on one side and the other goes deep blue, pushed toward
the scene's shadow colour rather than glowing at full saturation — a mascot
that stays bright while the world around him goes dark reads as pasted on. Snow
is kicked up around the lifted foot, and he casts a soft contact shadow;
without one he hovers.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The firs, the spilled warm light and the mascot belong in the middle and the
right, with the mascot against plain snow bank rather than a busy tangle of
boughs. The left side of the picture is open drifted snow and a few dark trunks
the warm light never reaches — the light comes from off-frame right, so it
simply does not carry that far.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Cold blue night against one warm off-frame source from the right. Snow reads
blue and violet in shadow, gold only where the light lands. Very low key
overall. Accent hues: lantern amber #d07400 in the spilled light, periwinkle
#6467dc through the shadowed snow.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss. The
mascot is painted with the same brush as the snow — same light, same edge
quality — not dropped in as a sticker or a cartoon overlay.
