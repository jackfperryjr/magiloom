SCENE 04 — THREE MOONS  (key: moons, any season, night only)
============================================================

SUBJECT
Three moons in one night sky, arcing across the upper half of the frame at
different heights. They are three different bodies, and getting their colours,
sizes and phases apart is the whole point of this scene.

The leftmost of the three is a dull grey-violet moon, #8d8798, the coldest and
least reflective of them, middle-sized, and shown as a thin crescent barely a
fifth lit — most of its disc unlit and only just separable from the sky. The
middle one is pale blue-white, #8fb6e8, the largest of the three and the
brightest thing in the picture, nearly full: just short of a perfect circle,
its terminator a shallow curve near one edge. The rightmost is a dusty
rose-red, #d06a6a, muted rather than blood bright, the smallest of the three,
and gibbous at about two-thirds lit.

Each reads as a sphere, not a flat disc: visible maria as broad soft darkening,
craters with a dark floor and a lit rim on the sunward side, and limb darkening
toward the edge. All three are lit from the same direction, which is what sells
them as three bodies in one sky.

Thin cloud banks drift in front of them. Below, only a pair of soft ridgelines
for a horizon, the far one lighter than the near one. A dense field of stars
behind everything, and one faint shooting star, small, off to one side.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
All three moons sit across the middle and the right of the sky, in the order
given above, none of them out at the far left. The near-left is open sky and
thin cloud: no moon, no bright star. Leave the extreme bottom edge simple and
unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Deep indigo and blue-black sky, graduating slightly warmer toward the horizon.
The moons keep the three colours given above — grey-violet, blue-white,
rose-red — and they must stay distinguishable from each other at a glance. The
brightest value in the frame is the middle moon's lit face; nothing else
competes with it. Accent hues, used sparingly: periwinkle #6467dc and lantern
amber #d07400.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.
