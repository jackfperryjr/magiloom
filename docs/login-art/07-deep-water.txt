SCENE 07 — DEEP WATER  (key: deep, any season, any time of day)
===============================================================

SUBJECT
Underwater, well below the surface, looking up and out. The surface is overhead
as a rippling ceiling seen from beneath; caustics — crossing bands of light —
run along it and scatter down. Long shafts of light come through at an angle
and waver out of phase with each other. Marine snow drifts everywhere: fine
suspended motes, brighter where the shafts catch them, which is what tells you
this is a volume of water you are inside of rather than a flat blue field.

The sea floor is a dark shelf across the bottom of the frame, mostly
featureless, with the water going black in the corners. No fish, no wreck, no
diver — the subject is the light and the water.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The strongest light shafts and the brightest caustics fall through the middle
and the right. The left is open water the shafts never reach — clear, empty,
deepening blue with a little marine snow drifting in it. Leave the extreme
bottom edge simple and unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
One source, from above and to the right. Teal and blue-green near the surface
falling to near-black blue at depth. The caustics are the brightest thing in
the frame and they are thin, not broad. Accent hues, used sparingly: periwinkle
#6467dc and lantern amber #d07400 (the latter only in the surface light).

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.

NEGATIVE
No text or lettering of any kind, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no
border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle
of flat colour over any part of the image, no vignette, no darkened band or
shaded column down one side, no gradient overlay across the frame, no
photographic texture, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no HDR glow, no
vector-flat shapes, no people or faces, no sea creatures, no bubbles rising in
a stream. No labels, captions, names, numbers, arrows or leader lines anywhere
in the picture — this is a painting, not a diagram.
