SCENE 06 — FORGE  (key: forge, any season, any time of day)
===========================================================

SUBJECT
The inside of a smithy, lit only by its own coals. The forge itself is below
the frame, so what you see is the glow coming up from the bottom edge and the
sparks it throws — embers rising in a slow drifting column, hot yellow-white at
the bottom, orange in the middle, dull red as they cool and die near the top.

Everything else is silhouette: a dark bench, hanging tongs and hammers, a low
sooty ceiling above, the far wall lost entirely. A single dark mass across the
very bottom of the frame, curving slightly, reads as the near edge of the forge
between camera and the coals.

Nobody is working it. The room is warm and still and just been left.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The ember column rises through the middle and the right. The left is the cold
end of the room: bare wall and dark air the fire barely reaches, with no tools,
no bench and no glow over there. Leave the extreme bottom edge simple and
unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
One light source, from below and out of frame. Deep purple-black in the upper
corners (#0b0818 to #170f24), warming through wine and ember-red to a hot amber
core at the bottom edge. Nothing in the frame is neutral grey — every shadow
carries the fire's colour. Accent hues: lantern amber #d07400 leading, with
periwinkle #6467dc only in the coldest upper corners.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.

NEGATIVE
No text or lettering of any kind, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no
border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle
of flat colour over any part of the image, no vignette, no darkened band or
shaded column down one side, no gradient overlay across the frame, no
photographic texture, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no HDR glow, no
vector-flat shapes, no people or faces. Do not show the fire itself — only what
it lights. No labels, captions, names, numbers, arrows or leader lines anywhere
in the picture — this is a painting, not a diagram.
