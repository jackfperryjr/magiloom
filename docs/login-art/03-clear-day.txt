SCENE 03 — CLEAR DAY  (key: clearDay, summer, daytime only)
===========================================================

SUBJECT
A wide green country under a big summer sky, late afternoon rather than blazing
noon. Rolling grassland running back to a hazy horizon, a few fair-weather
clouds with flat bases and piled tops drifting high, and the light coming in
low and warm from the right so the ground carries long soft shadows.

This is the only scene in the set that is genuinely bright, and it still has to
stay a stop under what feels natural — so treat it as evening gold, not midday
glare. The haze at the horizon should be strong enough that nothing cuts hard.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The sun's warmth, the clouds and the deepest distance belong in the middle and
the right. The near-left is plain grassland on the shadowed side of the light —
the sun is off to the right, so nothing over there catches it and no cloud edge
lights up. Leave the extreme bottom edge simple and unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Warm low sun from the right; deep green and olive in shadow, gold on the grass
heads. Sky graduating from a dusty blue overhead to pale warm haze at the
horizon. Keep the overall exposure a stop below what feels natural. Accent
hues, used sparingly: periwinkle #6467dc and lantern amber #d07400.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.

NEGATIVE
No text or lettering of any kind, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no
border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle
of flat colour over any part of the image, no vignette, no darkened band or
shaded column down one side, no gradient overlay across the frame, no
photographic texture, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no HDR glow, no
vector-flat shapes, no people or faces. No labels, captions, names, numbers,
arrows or leader lines anywhere in the picture — this is a painting, not a
diagram.
