SCENE 13 — CLEAR NIGHT  (key: clearNight, night only)
=====================================================

SUBJECT
A clear, moonless night sky over open country. Thousands of stars, mostly faint
pinpricks with a handful of real standouts — uniform brightness reads as
static, so vary it hard. A denser drift of stars runs diagonally across the
upper frame standing in for the galactic band, softer and more granular than
the individual stars around it, with a couple of dark dust lanes through it.

Below, a faint airglow along the horizon, and a low dark landform — hills, a
treeline — as the simplest possible silhouette. Nothing else. This is the quiet
scene in the set.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The galactic band brightens as it crosses the middle and the right, and the
strongest stars are there. It enters thin and faint at the upper left. The left
side of the picture is sparse, quiet sky with no standout stars in it. Leave
the extreme bottom edge simple and unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
No moon, no artificial light. Sky graduating from near-black blue-violet
overhead (#05061a) through deep indigo (#0b1030) to a slightly lifted blue at
the horizon (#2b3162). Stars in cold white with a few warm amber and pale blue
ones scattered among them. Accent hues, used sparingly: periwinkle #6467dc and
lantern amber #d07400.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.
