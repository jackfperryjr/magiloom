SCENE 10 — HALLOWS  (key: hallows, holiday scene, night only)
=============================================================

SUBJECT
All Hallows night, with the mascot out in it.

A low, bloated moon hangs near the horizon, pale and sickly, with a couple of
dark maria across it so it is not a flat disc. Bare crooked trees stand in
silhouette. Ground fog drifts across the field in slabs, thicker in some bands
than others, swallowing the base of everything. Two or three jack-o'-lanterns
sit low in the middle distance, their carved faces guttering — the light inside
them is unsteady, throwing a weak orange glow onto the fog immediately around
each one and no further.

THE MASCOT
At about 83% across the frame, standing in the fog with his feet near the
bottom edge and about a third of the frame tall: a small round-limbed ragdoll
made of stitched amber-gold cloth (#e8a72c body, #c98720 in shadow, warm
#f7cf6a highlights, dark brown stitching), with a large round head nearly half
his total height, visible seams along his limbs, and a soft sheen high on one
side of the head. Soft, hand-sewn, a little lopsided — a toy, not a creature.

HE HAS NO EYES AND NO FACE. His only feature is a short row of stitch ticks low
on the head, curving slightly, which reads as a small closed smile. Nothing
else is marked on the head at all — no eyes, no button eyes, no nose, no brows.

He is trying to be frightening. Both arms are raised in front of him, elbows
bent and paws hanging loose, shoulders hunched and head thrust forward in a
small clumsy lunge — the pose of something going "boo". He wears a black
pointed witch's hat with a soft bend near the tip, tipped forward over the
lunge. He is a soft toy with no face, so the attempt is entirely endearing
rather than menacing; paint the effort, not the threat.

He is lit by the same weak lantern glow as the fog and pushed well toward the
scene's shadow colour, never glowing at full saturation — a mascot that stays
bright while the world around him goes dark reads as pasted on. The nearest
jack-o'-lantern catches him low, from in front, so the light comes up under him
— that underlighting is doing far more of the spooky work than he is. Fog laps
around his feet and he casts a faint contact shadow; without one he hovers.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The jack-o'-lanterns, the moon and the crooked trees all belong in the middle
and the right, with the mascot clear of them and no lantern or trunk directly
behind him. The left side of the picture is open field running away into fog:
no lantern, no trunk, no moon — nothing but drifting fog and dead grass. It is
the emptiest part of the field, painted at the same exposure as the rest of it.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Two weak sources fighting: cold moonlight from behind, warm guttering orange
from the lanterns in front. Everything between them is desaturated grey-green
fog. Very low key — this is the darkest scene in the set. Accent hues: lantern
amber #d07400 in the pumpkin glow, periwinkle #6467dc in the moonlit fog.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss. The
mascot is painted with the same brush as the fog — same light, same edge
quality — not dropped in as a sticker or a cartoon overlay.

NEGATIVE
No text or lettering of any kind, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no
border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle
of flat colour over any part of the image, no vignette, no darkened band or
shaded column down one side, no gradient overlay across the frame, no
photographic texture, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no HDR glow, no
vector-flat shapes, no gore, no ghosts, no gravestones, nothing cartoonishly
spooky. No human figures and no human faces, and NO EYES on the mascot — no
button eyes, no dots, no stitched crosses for eyes. His head carries nothing
but the small stitched smile. No labels, captions, names, numbers, arrows or
leader lines anywhere in the picture — this is a painting, not a diagram.
