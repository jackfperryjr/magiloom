SCENE 05 — GROVE  (key: grove, autumn, any time of day)
=======================================================

SUBJECT
An autumn wood in a steady wind. Bare-ish deciduous trees in three depth
layers, all leaning the same way in one gust — trunks barely moving, crowns
bending, the outermost twigs whipping. Torn leaves stream through the air on
that same wind, in rust, amber and dull gold. Leaf litter over the ground
below.

Atmospheric perspective does the work: the near trees are dark, warm and
detailed; the middle distance is greyer; the furthest trees are little more
than blue-grey shapes in haze. Overcast light, no visible sun.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The big near trunks and the busiest branch tangle belong right of centre. The
left side of the picture falls away into distance — hazy blue-grey trees with
no detail in them, because they are far off, not because they have been dimmed.
Leave the extreme bottom edge simple and unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Flat overcast light, cool grey-blue in the distance against warm rust and ochre
in the foreground. Low-key overall. Accent hues, used sparingly: periwinkle
#6467dc and lantern amber #d07400.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.

NEGATIVE
No text or lettering of any kind, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no
border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle
of flat colour over any part of the image, no vignette, no darkened band or
shaded column down one side, no gradient overlay across the frame, no
photographic texture, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no HDR glow, no
vector-flat shapes, no people or faces. No path or road leading the eye out of
frame. No labels, captions, names, numbers, arrows or leader lines anywhere in
the picture — this is a painting, not a diagram.
