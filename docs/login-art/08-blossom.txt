SCENE 08 — BLOSSOM  (key: blossom, spring, daytime only)
========================================================

SUBJECT
An orchard in full bloom, early on a spring morning — a place, seen from within
it. A grass lane runs between two rows of old flowering trees, their trunks
gnarled and leaning, their crowns meeting overhead so the lane passes under a
loose canopy of blossom. The rows recede into soft distance, and past the last
of them a low field and a line of hills fade into morning haze.

The blossom grows in clusters on the wood, not scattered singly: five round
petals around a small yellow centre, in warm white and the palest pink, thick
enough on some boughs to hide the branch. Fallen petals lie drifted along the
lane and caught in the grass. More drift down through the still air, turning as
they fall.

Somebody keeps this place — a dry-stone wall along one side, a worn path in the
grass — but nobody is in it this early.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
Eye level, standing in the lane. The lane and the deepest recession of the rows
belong in the middle and the right, so the picture opens up that way. The left
is a near trunk and plain grass in the shade of it — no bright blossom cluster,
no sunlit petal, nothing on that side worth stopping at. Leave the extreme
bottom edge simple and unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Soft diffuse early light, no hard sun and no cast shadows with edges. Blossom
in warm white and pale pink; wood in cool grey-brown; grass a muted spring
green; the distance a hazy green-blue. Keep the overall exposure low for the
season — this is first light, not midday. Accent hues, used sparingly:
periwinkle #6467dc and lantern amber #d07400.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss.
