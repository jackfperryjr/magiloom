SCENE 09 — HARVEST  (key: harvest, holiday scene, any time of day)
==================================================================

SUBJECT
A ripe wheat field under a harvest moon, with the mascot standing in it.

The moon is big, low and amber, sitting just above the horizon and dominating
the sky — the kind that looks impossibly large. The wheat fills the lower half
of the frame, heavy-headed, all of it leaning together on one gust, with the
grain heads catching the moonlight along their upper edges. A dark treeline and
a couple of distant rooftops sit between the field and the horizon. Warm,
still, late-autumn evening.

THE MASCOT
At about 82% across the frame, standing in the wheat with his feet near the
bottom edge and about a third of the frame tall: a small round-limbed ragdoll
made of stitched amber-gold cloth (#e8a72c body, #c98720 in shadow, warm
#f7cf6a highlights, dark brown stitching), with a large round head nearly half
his total height, visible seams along his limbs, and a soft sheen high on one
side of the head. Soft, hand-sewn, a little lopsided — a toy, not a creature.

HE HAS NO EYES AND NO FACE. His only feature is a short row of stitch ticks low
on the head, curving slightly, which reads as a small closed smile. Nothing
else is marked on the head at all — no eyes, no button eyes, no nose, no brows.

He stands in three-quarter view looking out over the field, wearing a plain
black pilgrim hat with a buckle.

He is lit by the same moon as everything else and pushed toward the scene's own
shadow colour, never glowing at full saturation — a mascot that stays bright
while the world around him goes dark reads as pasted on. He casts a soft
contact shadow where he meets the ground; without one he hovers. The wheat
closes over his feet, so the field's foreground passes in front of him.

FORMAT

The moon sits right of centre, roughly two-thirds across, and the mascot stands
clear of it. The left side of the picture is open wheat running away to the
treeline — no bright grain heads, nothing standing in it. Nothing in the frame

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to

horizon; the wheat in bronze and old gold where it is lit and near-black where
