SCENE 01 — DOWNPOUR  (key: downpour, any season, any time of day)
=================================================================

SUBJECT
Hard rain falling through the dark, with the mascot out in it under an umbrella.
A street-level view of a wet stone road on a storm night: slanting rain drawn in
long thin streaks, the ground slick and mirror-black, standing water throwing
back what little light there is. A single warm lantern hangs off-frame to the
left so its glow pools on the wet ground below without the lamp itself being
visible. Distant buildings and rooftops are only silhouettes behind the rain.

The picture is about volume of falling water — the rain should read as a moving
curtain with depth, denser and softer far away, sharp and separated close to
camera.

THE MASCOT
At about 82% across the frame, standing on the wet road with his feet near the
bottom edge and about a third of the frame tall: a small round-limbed ragdoll
made of stitched amber-gold cloth (#e8a72c body, #c98720 in shadow, warm #f7cf6a
highlights, dark brown stitching), with a large round head nearly half his total
height, visible seams along his limbs, and a soft sheen high on one side of the
head. Soft, hand-sewn, a little lopsided — a toy, not a creature.

HE HAS NO EYES AND NO FACE. His only feature is a short row of stitch ticks low
on the head, curving slightly, which reads as a small closed smile. Nothing else
is marked on the head at all — no eyes, no button eyes, no nose, no brows.

He holds up a small dark umbrella in one hand, its canopy tipped slightly into
the weather and far too big for him, water running off the ribs in threads and
bouncing off the fabric in a fine spray. His free arm hangs at his side. He is
bare-headed — no hat in this scene — and perfectly content, standing in the rain
because the rain is worth standing in.

He is lit by the same cold light as the street, warmed a little where the
lantern's pool reaches him, and pushed toward the scene's shadow colour rather
than glowing at full saturation — a mascot that stays bright while the world
around him goes dark reads as pasted on. The umbrella keeps his head and
shoulders dry, so the cloth low on his legs is darker where it has soaked
through. He stands in shallow standing water with a smeared reflection under
him, and casts a soft contact shadow; without one he hovers.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
The lantern glow on the wet stone, the heaviest rain and the rooftops behind it
belong in the middle and the right, with the mascot standing clear in the pooled
light. The near-left is open wet road running away empty — no lamplight reaching
that far, no reflections worth looking at. Leave the extreme bottom edge simple
and unbusy.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Low-key and dark overall. The brightest value in the frame is a small
highlight, not a broad area, and it sits with the subject. Cold slate blues and
rain-grey, warmed only where the lantern light lands on the wet stone. Accent
hues, used sparingly: periwinkle #6467dc and lantern amber #d07400.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss. The
mascot is painted with the same brush as the street — same light, same edge
quality, same rain falling on him — not dropped in as a sticker or a cartoon
overlay.

NEGATIVE
No text or lettering of any kind, no logo, no watermark, no signature, no
border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle
of flat colour over any part of the image, no vignette, no darkened band or
shaded column down one side, no gradient overlay across the frame, no
photographic texture, no lens flare, no chromatic aberration, no HDR glow, no
vector-flat shapes. No human figures and no human faces, and NO EYES on the
mascot — no button eyes, no dots, no stitched crosses for eyes. His head carries
nothing but the small stitched smile. No labels, captions, names, numbers,
arrows or leader lines anywhere in the picture — this is a painting, not a
diagram.
