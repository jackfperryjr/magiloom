SCENE 12 — FIREWORKS  (key: fireworks, holiday scene, night only)
=================================================================

SUBJECT
A fireworks night over open ground, with the mascot watching them.

Two shells are already open in the upper half of the frame at different heights
and sizes — spherical bursts whose sparks have begun to arc over and trail,
each burst a single colour (warm gold, rose, cold blue, violet or pale green;
do not mix hues inside one burst). A third shell is still climbing from below,
a thin bright streak with a smoke trail behind it. Below, only a dark horizon
line and a low silhouetted treeline. A field of stars fills the sky behind the
smoke, dimmer near the bursts.

THE MASCOT
At about 82% across the frame, standing on the dark ground with his feet near
the bottom edge and about a third of the frame tall: a small round-limbed
ragdoll made of stitched amber-gold cloth (#e8a72c body, #c98720 in shadow,
warm #f7cf6a highlights, dark brown stitching), with a large round head nearly
half his total height and visible seams along his limbs. Soft, hand-sewn, a
little lopsided — a toy, not a creature.

HE HAS NO EYES AND NO FACE. His only feature is a short row of stitch ticks low
on the head, curving slightly, which reads as a small closed smile — and seen
from behind here, even that is hidden. Nothing is marked on the head at all —
no eyes, no button eyes, no nose, no brows.

He is seen from behind in three-quarter view, head tipped up to watch the
bursts, one arm raised in a wave, wearing a tall striped top hat in red, white
and blue.

He is lit almost entirely by the fireworks, so he is mostly silhouette with
coloured rim light down one edge — never glowing at full saturation, which
would read as pasted on. He casts a faint contact shadow on the ground; without
one he hovers.

FORMAT
16:9 landscape, 2560x1440, full-bleed. A pure background painting for a fantasy
game's sign-in screen: nothing but the scene itself, edge to edge, with no
graphic or interface elements of any kind in the image.

COMPOSITION
Both open bursts belong right of centre and high in the frame — above the
mascot, not behind him, so his silhouette stays clean against dark sky. The
left side of the picture is empty night sky and drifting smoke, holding nothing
brighter than a few faint stars.

The picture is one continuous scene, evenly lit and evenly painted from edge to
edge. Do not divide it into zones, and do not paint a band, column, ribbon,
panel, frame or box anywhere in it; do not darken, shade, tint, blur or
vignette one side relative to the other. The quiet side is quiet because
nothing is happening there, not because something has been laid over it.

LIGHT AND PALETTE
Near-black night sky. The bursts are the only real light and they are made of
thin bright lines, not glowing blobs — the sparks read as individual trails.
Their light barely reaches the ground. Accent hues: lantern amber #d07400 and
periwinkle #6467dc are good choices for the two burst colours.

STYLE
Painterly digital illustration with visible brushwork and soft cinematic
lighting. Part of a set with painted character portraits, so the same hand:
rich but restrained colour, forms read at a glance, no rendering fuss. The
mascot is painted with the same brush as the rest of the scene — same light,
same edge quality — not dropped in as a sticker or a cartoon overlay.

border or frame, no panel, no card, no ribbon, no banner, no box, no rectangle

No human figures and no human faces, and NO EYES on the mascot — no button
eyes, no dots, no stitched crosses for eyes. His head carries nothing but the
small stitched smile. No labels, captions, names, numbers, arrows or leader
